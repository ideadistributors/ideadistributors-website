IDEA DISTRIBUTORS — PREMIUM WEBSITE

PREVIEW
Open index.html in a browser.

PUBLISH ON CLOUDFLARE PAGES
1. Sign in to Cloudflare.
2. Open Compute (Workers & Pages).
3. Choose Create > Pages > Upload assets.
4. Project name: idea-distributors
5. Upload the entire contents of this folder.
6. Deploy.
7. Add custom domains:
   ideadistributors.com
   www.ideadistributors.com

NOTES
- The website is bilingual (English / Spanish).
- The quote form opens the visitor's email app and prepares a message to:
  isaac@ideadistributors.com
- All graphics and logo assets are included locally.
- No external libraries or paid hosting are required.
